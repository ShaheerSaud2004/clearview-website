How It Works

From booking to report in four steps.

Booking, the inspection agreement and payment all happen online, so there's no paperwork to chase.

  1. Book online

    Pick a time that works for you and your agent. Sign the agreement and pay before or after the inspection. Payment just needs to be in before your report is released.

  2. Inspection day

    Most homes take 2–4 hours. We encourage you to come for the whole inspection, or at least the last part, and we'll walk through what we found together.

  3. Your report

    A digital report with photos and plain-language notes, delivered within 24–48 hours.

  4. Questions after

    Call or text with anything that comes up during negotiations, or after you move in.

Before inspection day

A few things that keep the inspection complete. Most sellers' agents handle these, but it's worth checking.

  • Water, gas and electric are on, and pilot lights are lit
  • Clear access to the attic hatch, electrical panel, furnace and water heater
  • Garage, crawlspace, gates and outbuildings are unlocked
  • Pets are secured or out of the house

What We Inspect

Twelve systems, checked on every inspection.

Tap a number on the house or a system below.

Outside01 / 12

Roofing

Your roof is the first line of defense against water. We inspect every slope by drone, so nothing is missed and no shingles are damaged by walking on them.

What we check

  • Shingles and roof covering
  • Flashing at chimneys, vents and valleys
  • Gutters, downspouts and drainage
  • Signs of past leaks and wear
  • Drone photos of every slope

Common findings

  • Worn, lifted or missing shingles
  • Loose or rusted flashing
  • Clogged gutters or short downspouts

Outside02 / 12

Exterior

How water moves around the house matters as much as the house itself. We walk the whole exterior and the lot around it.

What we check

  • Siding, trim and exterior paint
  • Grading and drainage away from the house
  • Decks, porches, steps and railings
  • Walkways and driveway
  • Exterior doors and windows

Common findings

  • Soil sloping toward the foundation
  • Rotted trim or siding
  • Loose deck railings or steps

Structure03 / 12

Structure

We look for anything that suggests the frame of the house is moving, overloaded or was changed without care.

What we check

  • Visible floor, wall and roof framing
  • Beams, posts and supports
  • Signs of sagging or settlement
  • Past repairs and modifications

Common findings

  • Cracked, cut or notched joists
  • Sloped or bouncy floors
  • Undersized or missing supports

Structure04 / 12

Foundation & basement

The foundation carries the whole house. We check for cracks, movement and water, the most common and costly basement problems.

What we check

  • Foundation walls and floors
  • Cracks and signs of movement
  • Moisture, staining and efflorescence
  • Crawlspaces where accessible
  • Sump pump and drainage

Common findings

  • Water staining or active moisture
  • Settlement or step cracks
  • Sump pump issues or missing lid

Systems05 / 12

Electrical

We open the main panel and test a representative sample of outlets, looking for anything unsafe or out of date.

What we check

  • Service entrance and meter
  • Main panel, breakers and grounding
  • Wiring type and condition
  • Outlets, switches and GFCI / AFCI protection
  • Smoke and CO detectors

Common findings

  • Double-tapped or wrong-size breakers
  • Missing GFCI protection near water
  • Open junction boxes or exposed splices

Systems06 / 12

Plumbing

From the water heater to every faucet, we run the water and look for leaks, poor drainage and aging parts.

What we check

  • Water supply lines and pressure
  • Drains, waste and vent piping
  • Fixtures, faucets and toilets
  • Water heater age, venting and safety valve
  • Visible leaks and corrosion

Common findings

  • Water heater near the end of its life
  • Slow or poorly vented drains
  • Leaks under sinks and around toilets

Systems07 / 12

HVAC

We run the heating and cooling with normal controls and check that air actually reaches the rooms.

What we check

  • Furnace or boiler operation
  • Air conditioning and heat pump
  • Ductwork and air distribution
  • Thermostat and normal controls
  • Filters, flues and venting

Common findings

  • Dirty filters and poor airflow
  • Aging equipment past typical lifespan
  • Rooms with weak or no supply air

Systems08 / 12

Ventilation

Moisture that can not escape turns into mold and rot. We follow where air from the attic, baths and kitchen goes.

What we check

  • Attic ventilation, soffits and ridge vents
  • Bathroom exhaust fans
  • Kitchen exhaust
  • Dryer venting

Common findings

  • Bath fans venting into the attic
  • Blocked soffit vents
  • Dryer vents that are too long or crushed

Inside09 / 12

Interior

We walk every room, opening doors and windows and looking for clues of water damage or wear.

What we check

  • Walls, ceilings and floors
  • Windows and doors open, close and lock
  • Stairs, handrails and guards
  • Signs of water damage and staining

Common findings

  • Ceiling stains from past leaks
  • Fogged windows with failed seals
  • Loose or missing handrails

Inside10 / 12

Attic

The attic often tells the story of the roof. We enter where it is safe and check the framing and sheathing.

What we check

  • Roof framing and sheathing
  • Stains, daylight and signs of leaks
  • Signs of pests
  • Ventilation paths

Common findings

  • Stained or damaged sheathing
  • Evidence of rodents or insects
  • Mold from poor ventilation

Inside11 / 12

Insulation

Good insulation keeps energy bills down. We note the type and depth and where coverage is missing.

What we check

  • Insulation type and approximate depth
  • Gaps and uneven coverage
  • Vapor barriers where visible
  • Attic hatch insulation

Common findings

  • Thin or uneven insulation
  • Uninsulated attic hatch
  • Insulation blocking soffit vents

Inside12 / 12

Installed systems

Fireplaces and the built-in appliances that come with the house are tested so there are no surprises on move-in day.

What we check

  • Fireplaces, dampers and visible chimney
  • Range, oven, dishwasher and disposal
  • Built-in microwave and range hood
  • Garage door opener and safety reverse

Common findings

  • Damper that does not close
  • Garage door that does not auto-reverse
  • Appliances that do not work

What does a home inspection include?

A visual, non-invasive evaluation of the home's major systems and components: roof, exterior, structure, foundation, electrical, plumbing, heating and cooling, interior, attic, insulation and ventilation. You'll get a detailed digital report with photos and recommendations.

How long does an inspection take?

Most homes take about 2–4 hours. Larger or older homes, or added services, can take longer.

Should the buyer attend?

Yes, we encourage it. Joining us for the whole inspection, or at least the last part, lets us walk you through what we found and answer your questions in person.

When will I receive my report?

Reports are delivered within 24–48 hours as an easy-to-read digital report you can share with your agent. Payment needs to be complete before the report is released.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Phillipsburg, NJ and serve all of New Jersey, including Warren, Hunterdon, Sussex, Morris, Somerset, Passaic, Bergen, Essex, Union, Hudson, Middlesex, Mercer, Monmouth, Burlington, Camden and Ocean counties. See the service area.

Do you offer radon testing?

Yes. Omer is a New Jersey certified Radon Measurement Technician (MET# 16008). Radon testing can be added to your home inspection or booked on its own.

Do you offer WDI inspections?

WDI (termite) inspections are coming soon. Contact us and we'll let you know as soon as they're available.

Do you inspect roofs?

Yes. The roof is part of every home inspection. Omer is a licensed drone pilot, so instead of walking on the roof, he inspects it by drone. You get a close, detailed look at every slope without anyone damaging shingles or taking unnecessary risks.

How do I schedule?

Click Schedule an Inspection to book online, or call or text . Scheduling, the inspection agreement and payment are all handled online.

How much does an inspection cost?

Pricing depends on the home's size, age and the services you need. Request a quote and you'll get a clear price up front, with no surprises.
