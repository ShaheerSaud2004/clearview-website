Home Inspections in New Jersey

A thorough, visual evaluation of the home's major systems, explained clearly so you can make your decision with confidence.

Schedule an Inspection Request a Quote

Who it's for

Buyers before closing, sellers who want to get ahead of surprises, and homeowners who want a clear picture of their home's condition.

What's included

  • Roof coverings, flashing, gutters and drainage, inspected by drone
  • Exterior siding, trim, grading, decks and walkways
  • Foundation, basement, crawlspace and visible structure
  • Electrical service, panel, outlets and GFCI protection
  • Plumbing supply, drains, fixtures and water heater
  • Heating and cooling systems
  • Interior walls, ceilings, floors, windows and doors
  • Attic, insulation and ventilation
  • Fireplaces, chimneys and specified kitchen and built-in appliances

How it works

  1. Schedule onlinePick a time that works with your contract deadlines.
  2. Sign the agreement & payHandled online, before or after the inspection. Payment must be in before your report is released.
  3. InspectionMost homes take 2–4 hours. We encourage you to join for the whole inspection, or at least the walkthrough at the end.
  4. Clear reportA digital report with photos, findings and recommendations, within 24–48 hours.
