Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

ClearView Property Inspections ("ClearView", "we", "us") is a home and commercial inspection business based in Phillipsburg, New Jersey. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit clearviewpropertyinspectors.com, book an inspection or contact us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • When you book online. Booking is handled by Spectora, our scheduling and reporting software. You give your name, email, phone number, the property address and details about the inspection. Spectora collects this on our behalf.
  • When you call, text or email us. We receive your phone number or email address and whatever you choose to tell us.
  • During an inspection. We record information about the property, including photos, notes and findings, to prepare your report.
  • When you visit the website. Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically logs basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. Your browser also remembers one display setting for this site. We do not use advertising trackers or analytics tools.

How we use it

  • To schedule, perform and confirm your inspection.
  • To prepare and deliver your inspection report and answer your questions about it.
  • To send the inspection agreement and process payment.
  • To reply when you contact us.
  • To keep records required for our business and our licenses.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Service providers that help us run the business, such as Spectora (scheduling, agreements, payments and reports) and our website host (GitHub Pages). They may use your information only to provide their service to us.
  • People you ask us to share with. Your report goes to you. With your permission, we can also share it with your real estate agent, attorney or others involved in the sale.
  • When required by law, for example in response to a valid legal request.

Third-party services

The booking form on our site is provided by Spectora, and the site loads fonts from Google Fonts. These services receive basic technical information, such as your IP address, when the page loads, and handle it under their own privacy policies. Links to other websites are not covered by this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep inspection reports and related records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet our legal, licensing and insurance obligations. Other contact information is kept only as long as it is useful for answering your requests.

How we protect it

Our website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and we rely on reputable providers to store booking and report data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to records we must keep by law. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
