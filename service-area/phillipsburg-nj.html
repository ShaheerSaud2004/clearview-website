Local knowledge of Phillipsburg homes

Phillipsburg mixes century-old homes near downtown with newer construction in the surrounding Warren County townships. In the older homes, watch for original wiring, galvanized supply pipes, buried oil tanks and stone foundations. In newer builds, grading, drainage and attic ventilation are where problems tend to show up.
